# Terms

A summary, not legal advice: read the RBA's own words before relying on
them. Terms change; these were read on 19 November 2026.

From the Reserve Bank of Australia's copyright and disclaimer notice
([rba.gov.au/copyright](https://www.rba.gov.au/copyright/)):

- **Financial data and the cash rate** may be used, reproduced and
  published for personal or commercial use, provided no endorsement by the
  RBA is stated or implied.
- **Not allowed:** obtaining a benefit by deception, misrepresenting the
  data's source, or charging customers for access without telling them the
  RBA publishes it free of charge; any unlawful use.
- **Not a benchmark.** The RBA does not administer or recommend its
  financial data as a benchmark. The cash rate is administered under its
  Cash Rate Procedures Manual.
- **Attribution:** "Source: Reserve Bank of Australia" with the year; it
  must not suggest the RBA endorses you.
- **Third-party content** in the data may not be used without the third
  party's consent. The tables name these columns' sources: ASX (BABs/NCDs),
  FENICS (OIS) and WM/Reuters (A$1=USD).
- **Changes.** The RBA may withdraw or change the data, its methods or its
  schedule without notice.

**Served, not kept.** The third parties' columns (`rba_restricted`) are read
only when your lake wants a day, and the plugin keeps none of them. Their
terms carry the clause, and "served, not kept" is the licence default it
asks you to set.

Each dataset describes these terms in words, shown on the Data sources
page, and declares no licence. The licence a deployment admin sets there is
the one enforced; the plugin says nothing of whether your deployment meets
anyone's terms.

Australia.
