# Pages

Both pages are the data role's, under **Manage**, for the plugin's admins.
Neither shows a rate or a price. Each is also a read tool on the
deployment's MCP, answering what the page shows.

## Connection (`read_connection`)

The head's status; **Key** (the mode, "No key needed"); **Limits and use**
(one request at a time, at most one every two seconds, each table asked
every hour whether it changed; the requests made, the last check and
error); **Stream** (it does not stream); and **Sources**, a tab of its own:
F1, F2, F17 and F11.1, each with its state, latest day and calls, and in
its details where it reads and what of it is kept.

## Datasets (`read_datasets`)

**Served**: its four datasets as declared, whether each is licensed, what
it recorded and declined; **Wants**, a tab of its own: the wants it keeps
current and answered, and whether it may read an instrument's record; then
**Records**, a tab of its own: each record and exchange rate, what it is recorded as, its
convention, and your deployment's instrument for it or why there is none,
and each column read and not carried, searched and paged to fit one screen.
